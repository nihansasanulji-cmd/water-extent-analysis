About the Project

The Problem

Labugama and Kalatuwawa are reservoirs that feed water supply for the Colombo region. Changes in their surface water extent affect supply security, yet they are rarely tracked in a simple, visual and repeatable way.

Our Geospatial Approach

Landsat imagery from 2019 and 2026 is converted to NDWI to separate water from land, the water is delineated as polygons, and the two years are compared on this web map.

Potential Benefit

  • Gives water managers a low-cost way to monitor reservoir shrinkage or expansion.
  • Supports early planning for drought and supply risk.
  • The same workflow can be repeated for other reservoirs and years.

How to Use the Map

Toggle the 2019 and 2026 layers, adjust opacity to compare them, use the quick-zoom buttons for each reservoir, and click a water body for its details.

Study Area & Data

Study Area: Labugama & Kalatuwawa Reservoirs, Western Province, Sri Lanka

Data Source: Landsat multi-spectral imagery (2019 & 2026), courtesy of NASA/USGS

Methodology: NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index) extraction → polygon delineation → PAEK surface smoothing → Web GIS integration.
