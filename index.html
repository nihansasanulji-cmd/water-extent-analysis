Project Overview & Geospatial Solution

Study Area: Labugama & Kalatuwawa Reservoirs, Sri Lanka

Data Source: Landsat Multi-Spectral Imagery (2019 & 2026)

Methodology: NDWI Extraction $\rightarrow$ Thresholding $\rightarrow$ PAEK Surface Smoothing $\rightarrow$ Pairwise Dissolve $\rightarrow$ Web GIS Integration.

⚠️ Identified Real-World Problem

Multi-temporal surface water analysis (2019–2026) reveals noticeable surface water extent shrinkage (Kalatuwawa: -26.17%, Labugama: -6.60%), posing potential risks to the drinking water supply security of the Colombo Metropolitan Region.

💡 Proposed Geospatial Solution

An interactive Web GIS Decision-Support Application enabling water resource authorities (such as NWSDB) to continuously monitor reservoir dynamics, supporting proactive drought risk mitigation, catchment protection, and strategic water allocation.
